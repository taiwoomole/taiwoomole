About me

... prototyping the Future in Motion

I am a multidisciplinary Creative Producer and genuine generalist operating at the intersection of deep tech, cinematic filmmaking, and digital retention. Rooted in traditional cinematic craft from The Los Angeles Film School and backed by an MSc in Innovation, Creativity & Leadership from Bayes Business School, I don’t just execute shot lists—I treat content as an iterative innovation process. I bridge the gap between complex engineering and audience psychology, building high-impact visual narratives that amplify brand potential

The Collaborator – Humanoid Robotics Concept Film (Spec)

Challenge

Translate a core robotics philosophy ”amplifying human potential rather than replacing it” into a visceral, cinematic narrative. The goal was to visualise safe, advanced human-robot collaboration in an industrial setting, proving product value before a physical robot is even deployed.

Execution

Directed an end-to-end conceptual spec film utilising a hybrid pipeline of Generative AI, After Effects HUD motion design, and immersive sound engineering. Crafted a visual arc that shifts from micro-precision hardware tasks to macro-level human collaboration, anchoring the company's mission statement with high-impact kinetic typography to create a deeply emotional, brand-aligned manifesto.
